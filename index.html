Ryuhei Mizoguchi

PhD Student at Waseda University

I am a PhD student at the Graduate School of Education, Waseda University, working in the field of language assessment and testing. My research focuses on how teachers understand and use assessment (language assessment literacy), how raters are trained to judge L2 performance, and how rubrics can capture both content knowledge and language ability. I am also exploring how artificial intelligence (AI) can support learning-oriented assessment and feedback.

Current Positions

  • PhD Student, Waseda University
  • Research Intern, Equmenopolis
  • Part-time Lecturer, Tokyo Future University

Research Interests

  • Language Assessment Literacy
  • Speaking Assessment
  • Rater Training
  • Argument-based Validation
  • Assessing Topical Knowledge with Language Ability
  • AI-mediated Language Assessment
